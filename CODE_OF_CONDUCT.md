# Code of conduct

## The standard

Be direct about code and decent about people. Technical disagreement is welcome and expected;
it is how the work gets better. Contempt, harassment, and personal attacks are not, and they
get you removed regardless of the quality of your patches.

Concretely, in issues, pull requests, and reviews:

- Critique the work, not the person. "This breaks on empty input" is a review. "This is
  amateur" is not.
- Assume the other person had a reason. Ask what it was before rewriting their work.
- No harassment, discrimination, or unwelcome personal attention of any kind, including in
  private messages that start from a public thread here.
- Do not derail technical threads into unrelated disputes.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete contributions that violate this standard, and may block
accounts that repeat it. Serious or repeated violations result in a permanent ban from the
organization's repositories.

To report a problem, use the **Report content** option on the offending comment, which reaches
GitHub directly, or open a private advisory through the repository's Security tab if the
matter is sensitive.

Enforcement decisions are made by the maintainers and are not subject to public appeal
threads. We will not litigate them in issues.
